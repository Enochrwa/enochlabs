# EnochLabs — Business Overview

> One sentence: **EnochLabs is a Rwanda-based digital solutions business that helps small
> and growing businesses establish an online presence, improve their operations, and grow
> through affordable websites, business software, digital services, and ongoing technical
> support.**

## The problem

Small and growing businesses in Rwanda (shops, restaurants, hotels, salons, clinics,
schools, NGOs, startups) often have good products but limited access to technology:

- No professional website — relying entirely on WhatsApp/Facebook/word of mouth.
- Manual, notebook-based tracking of stock, sales, customers, and expenses.
- No affordable, trustworthy local partner for ongoing maintenance once a site exists.
- Lost customers because the business is hard to find or evaluate online.

The underlying problem isn't "businesses need websites" — it's that **many small
businesses have limited access to affordable technology that could help them operate more
efficiently, reach more customers, and grow.**

## The solution

EnochLabs starts as a **digital services business** — understand the client's real
problem first, then deliver the simplest appropriate solution — and grows into a
**software/SaaS company** as recurring problems are turned into reusable products.

### Service categories

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Business websites | Restaurant/hotel sites with menu, booking, WhatsApp, maps |
| E-commerce / product presentation | Catalog, search, WhatsApp ordering, later full checkout |
| Business management software | Inventory, sales, customers, expenses, invoicing, dashboards |
| Maintenance & support | Monthly retainers: content updates, fixes, monitoring |
| Individual services | Portfolios, CVs, presentations, document/data formatting |

### Engagement flow

1. **Identify** — client describes a problem ("no website", "can't track stock").
2. **Diagnose** — EnochLabs learns the business, its workflow, budget, and constraints.
3. **Recommend** — the simplest solution that solves the real problem.
4. **Build** — scoped to the client's requirements.
5. **Deploy & train** — ship it, show the client how to use it.
6. **Support** — ongoing maintenance subscription; a relationship, not a one-off.

### Revenue streams

- Website development (per project)
- Custom software development (per project)
- Monthly maintenance retainers
- SaaS subscriptions (as reusable products emerge from repeated client needs)
- Small digital services for individuals

### Long-term vision

Individual developer → digital services business → software development company →
SaaS/product company → technology company serving African businesses.

The signal for "build this as a product" is repetition: when the same request (e.g.
inventory management) comes from many clients, EnochLabs builds one product and sells
subscriptions instead of bespoke rebuilds each time.

### Primary channels

Google Search/Maps, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, GitHub (proof of technical capability),
and referrals — all pointed at the EnochLabs website as the central hub.
